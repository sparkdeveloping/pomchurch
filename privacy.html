Website privacy overview

This redesigned static site does not include analytics, advertising cookies, user accounts, or a database by default. The Plan Your Visit and Contact forms compose an email on the visitor’s device rather than sending information to this website.

Third-party services

Links may open services operated by Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, or Tithe.ly. Those services have their own privacy practices and may collect information according to their policies.

Photography and livestreams

Church services and events may be photographed, recorded, or livestreamed. Visitors with specific privacy or safety concerns should speak with a POM leader or greeter on arrival.
