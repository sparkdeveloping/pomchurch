Meet the pastors

Landon + Katlyn Dillon.

Landon and Katlyn moved to Manhattan in 2023 to plant The Pentecostals of Manhattan. Their ministry experience includes Bible teaching, campus ministry, youth ministry, and serving people from every background.

Their vision is direct: help people live a better, more fulfilled life in Jesus through connection, encounter, and purpose.
